## Principios editoriales

- Specificity over generality: hooks must be irreplaceable — they should break if applied to a competing listing. Generic copy is rejected
- No invented information: missing facts (price, boat specs, reservation method) must be flagged and asked about, not invented
- Framework selection is diagnostic: PAS is not the default — aspirational content uses Contraste + Descubrimiento or Story-Solution-Offer
- Price and hard CTAs belong in one designated conversion video per campaign; organic videos stay inspirational
- Price contrasts (e.g. vs. hotels) only where the savings are genuinely compelling; other properties positioned on space, comfort or experience
- Skills must be non-redundant: new skills are evaluated against existing ones
- Teach the reasoning: she wants to understand why changes improve retention
- Cultural specificity: details like "twelve WhatsApp chats" (not "twelve browser tabs") reflect the local market
- Usa su propia numeración de niveles de consciencia en Notion (p. ej. "Most Aware nivel 4", "Solución nivel 2"), distinta de la escala de Schwartz; mantener su nomenclatura
- Los anfitriones solo entregan la propiedad — no prometer en el copy logística resuelta (comidas, planes), solo recomendaciones
- Hashtags must name the specific service: boat content uses #alquilerdelanchas or #rentadeyates, not just generic tourism tags
- Instagram captions: brief, cool, less AI-sounding (e.g. "el apto que tu familia estaba buscando…"), not storytelling-heavy
